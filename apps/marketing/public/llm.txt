# MeetMyPets

> MeetMyPets is a multi-species pet social ecosystem connecting pets and pet parents through verified playdates, companionship matching, community packs, and a directory of vetted pet care businesses.

MeetMyPets (by XLU Technologies Private Limited) brings pets and their people closer together. Designed for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, and all companion animals, MeetMyPets replaces chaotic messaging groups and unverified listings with a trusted, identity-checked platform where every pet has a profile, verified vaccination records, and local discovery.

## Platform Capabilities

- **Pet Discovery & Local Radar**: Discover compatible pets nearby filtered by species, breed, energy level, temperament, and distance.
- **Verified Playdates**: Connect with loving pet parents and coordinate safe outdoor meetups and play sessions.
- **Trust & Verification**: Multi-factor identity checks and veterinary vaccination record verification badges before meetups occur.
- **Ethical Companion Matching**: Verified health screenings and pedigree verification for responsible companionship.
- **Pet Care & Business Directory**: Discover vetted veterinarians, groomers, pet-friendly cafes, boarding facilities, and trainers.
- **Community Packs & Local Events**: Join neighborhood pet walks, breed-specific groups, and local pet parent discussions.
